Our usual location, Jumping Bean on Elizabeth Avenue, has changed its hours for the winter, such that it closes at 7pm.

So, **we've returning to meetups on the third floor of the Queen Elizabeth II Library.**

Thankfully, there's still warm beverages to be had at the Jumping Bean location on the first floor, although the options are a bit more scarce than our previous location.

_And_, there's plenty of tables to sit at / move around to construct our classic 'big table' setup, so we'll be right at home.

If you have any ideas of other places the group can meet, feel free to join us on Discord and discuss in the events channel.
