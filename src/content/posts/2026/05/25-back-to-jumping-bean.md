The Jumping Bean on Elizabeth Avenue is back to its normal hours, so the Thursday meetup is heading back!

We've spent the last few months at the QEII Library, which has extended hours, but a subpar
spot for Coffee (An _Aramark_ Jumping Bean), and a weird parking situation.

**This Thursday we're back at Jumping Bean, 7pm - 9pm.**

If you're new and want to confirm the meetup is actually on for a given week, ask in #events on the Discord. Someone's usually checking in beforehand.
